0 mYour lamp is your life

Oil is breath

The Wick's lamp burns oil. When it runs dry, the dark takes you. Gather drifting glimmers to keep it lit.

Light is bait

Anglers hunt by light. Burn bright and you see everything, and everything sees you. Go dim and you slip past, but you're blind.

Flares, pings, nerve

Throw a flare and the hunters chase it instead of you. Ping sonar to see the walls, but anything listening turns toward the sound.

200 mThe storm took the flame

Merrow Light has burned every night since 1887. Last night a storm broke the lantern room, and the flame went over the rail, still burning, all the way down.

Mum's arm is in a sling, and the boats won't sail without the light. So Sprat, the keeper's kid, pulls the tarp off Gran's old diving bell, the Wick, and goes down after it. Down through the sunlit harbor, the kelp and the cliffs, and the ruins of an older lighthouse the sea took long ago.

Gran left bottles wherever she was frightened. With the century-old keepers' logs, they tell the story of the Carrow family, and of why no lamp dropped over the side at Merrow has ever washed up.

Cozy-eerie, not horror. Lonely, a little scary, and warm at the end.

1000 mControls

WASDor↑←↓→

Swim. The Wick drifts, so ease into turns.

Shifthold

Dim the lamp. You're nearly invisible, it barely burns oil, and you're nearly blind.

Qtoggle

Bright lamp. You see far, oil burns fast, and you are very visible.

Space

Flare. A decoy, because hunters chase the brightest light. You only have a few.

E

Ping. Sonar outlines the walls for two seconds. It's loud, though.

Gamepad

Stick or d-pad to swim. Left bumper to dim, right bumper for bright. A flares, B pings. On a phone? There's a touch stick.

Lantern buoys save your progress and mend your hull. Esc or P pauses. From the pause menu, R returns you to the last buoy.

Assist mode: want the story without the stress? Press A in the pause menu for no damage and endless oil. No judgement. The sea is big.
